# StayOps Heating Failure SOP

Category: heating
Owner: Guest Operations
Status: approved

## Purpose

This procedure defines how StayOps handles heating problems reported
during an active guest stay.

## Initial checks

When a guest reports that heating is not working:

1. Confirm the guest and active reservation.
2. Identify the affected property.
3. Ask whether heating is completely unavailable or only reduced.
4. Ask whether the thermostat has power and displays normally.
5. The guest may adjust normal thermostat controls.
6. Do not instruct the guest to open, dismantle, or repair boilers,
   electrical panels, radiators, or heating equipment.

## Heating unavailable

If heating is completely unavailable:

1. Search the operational knowledge base for the relevant procedure.
2. Create a heating operations task.
3. Escalate when the issue cannot be safely restored through normal
   guest-accessible controls.

## High-priority conditions

Use high priority when:

- the property has no functioning heating
- outdoor conditions make the property unsafe or unsuitable
- vulnerable guests may be affected
- the failure is worsening
- the guest cannot reasonably remain comfortable in the property

## Safety conditions

Treat the situation as a safety issue when:

- the guest smells gas
- there is smoke
- electrical burning smells are present
- equipment is leaking significantly
- there are unusual sparks or exposed electrical components

Do not instruct the guest to investigate dangerous equipment.

The guest should move away from the affected equipment and human
intervention is required.

## Resolution

A heating incident is not resolved merely because a maintenance task
or escalation was created.

Resolution requires confirmation that:

- heating has been restored, or
- a safe temporary alternative has been provided, or
- the guest has been relocated when the property is not suitable
  for continued occupation.
