# StayOps Refund and Compensation SOP

Category: refund
Owner: Guest Operations
Status: approved

## Purpose

This procedure defines how StayOps handles guest requests for refunds,
credits, or financial compensation.

## Core rule

The Guest Operations Agent may gather information, explain the review
process, and escalate a compensation request.

The Guest Operations Agent is not authorized to approve, promise,
issue, or calculate final refunds or compensation.

Financial decisions require human approval.

## When a guest requests compensation

1. Confirm the guest and reservation.
2. Identify the operational issue connected to the request.
3. Review relevant incidents, tasks, and operational context.
4. Determine whether the issue has been documented.
5. Explain that the request will be reviewed.
6. Create or reuse a refund-category human escalation.

## Important restrictions

The agent must not:

- promise that a refund will be issued
- promise a specific amount
- state that compensation has been approved
- alter payment or booking financial records
- invent a company policy or refund percentage

## Human review

The human operator should receive:

- booking ID
- property ID
- reason for the request
- relevant operational issue
- severity and duration when known
- actions already taken
- guest's requested outcome

## Guest communication

The agent may tell the guest that the compensation request has been
submitted for review only after the escalation tool confirms that a
refund-category escalation exists.

## Resolution

A compensation request is not resolved until an authorized human
operator has made and recorded a decision.
