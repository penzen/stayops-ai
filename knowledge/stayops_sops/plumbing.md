# StayOps Plumbing Incident SOP

Category: plumbing
Owner: Guest Operations
Status: approved

## Purpose

This procedure defines how StayOps handles plumbing problems reported
during an active guest stay.

## Active water leak

When a guest reports an active or worsening water leak:

1. Confirm the guest and reservation.
2. Identify the affected property.
3. Ask the guest to stop using the affected fixture.
4. If safe and obvious, the guest may close the local shutoff valve.
5. The guest must not dismantle plumbing fixtures.
6. Keep electrical equipment and appliances away from water.
7. If water is approaching electrical outlets or equipment, instruct
   the guest to leave the affected area.
8. Create a plumbing operations task.
9. A worsening or uncontrolled leak requires a high-priority human
   escalation.
10. Do not claim that maintenance has been dispatched unless the
    relevant operational tool confirms it.

## Minor plumbing issue

Examples include:

- slow drain
- dripping faucet
- weak water pressure
- minor leak that is contained

Create a plumbing task.

Escalation priority may remain medium unless the condition is
worsening or affecting habitability.

## High-priority conditions

Use high priority when:

- water is actively spreading
- the leak is worsening
- water may reach electrical equipment
- significant property damage may occur
- a usable bathroom or kitchen is becoming unavailable

## Critical conditions

Treat as a safety situation when:

- water is contacting live electrical equipment
- flooding creates immediate danger
- structural damage appears possible

The guest should move away from the dangerous area and human
intervention is required.

## Resolution

A plumbing incident is not considered resolved merely because a task
or escalation was created.

Resolution requires confirmation that:

- the leak has stopped or been contained
- required repair work has been completed
- the property is safe for continued guest use
